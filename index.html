// Introduction

Greetings to whom might read this. My name is Clarence Nathan Lee Dizon Talanay, born as an accident on the 6th of April, 2006. Raised in Mabalacat and Angeles City. Most people know me as a quite outgoing person, but I know myself as a true loner who yearns for something I don't have nor will never have. But yes, I can be quite outgoing and loud sometimes, just as a coping mechanism.

I am a martial arts practicioner, specifically in boxing and wrestling. Though I focus on boxing much more since I'm an amatuer boxing athlete. I am also a visual artist, expert in both traditional and digital arts. As of now, I'm slowly losing my passion in all of these talents, might be because I'm not showing any improvement.

I can be sometimes hard to read since I can't properly express how I feel; I just tend to suppress my emotions in order to avoid bothering people. This habit of mine made me lose some people. Some died, some left me hanging, some are just walking around as if I'm a ghost, but that's fine by me. Besides, I have all my time in the world to enjoy.

As for my plan in the future, I plan to graduate, get a job (in legal terms), get rich, and buy a BMW S1000RR.
